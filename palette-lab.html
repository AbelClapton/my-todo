Sleep protocol

30 days · 4 studies reviewed · metric: sleep_hours

Across the four sources, the consistent finding is that a fixed wake time matters more than a fixed bedtime. The protocol below keeps that as the single non-negotiable.

InterventionEvidenceEffect
Fixed wake timeStrongSleep onset −18m
No screens after 22:00ModerateLatency −9m
Reading before bedWeakNot significant
Morning light within 30mStrongWake quality ↑

Your data covers 26 of 30 days. The two-week comparison shows a measurable improvement, and the one clear regression — the weekend drift — is what the review card will ask about.
