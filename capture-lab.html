Lab 11 · the first thing a user does

Capture

capture.md opens with the app's most important sentence: "capture is the first thing a user does and the moment where the app either feels effortless or feels like data entry." The rule it derives is capture without deciding.

The flow is unusually well specified across two docs — capture.md and tier-1-parsing.md state the same three confidence bands, the same thresholds, and the same fallback. That agreement is why nobody noticed that the three bands do not cover the range: there is a hole between 0.84 and 0.85, in both docs, in the one number that decides whether a parse applies itself.

The four entry points

capture.md §Invariants: "Capture is available from anywhere via the pull-down gesture." §The four entry points: "Pull-down. On any scrollable list, pull down to reveal the capture field at the top."

gesture-vocabulary.md's matrix assigns the Pull ↓ column to exactly five surfaces — Task list, Habit list, Note list, People list, Inbox — and gives both Calendar rows a dash. So "anywhere" is three modes and a filter, and the app's home surface has no capture gesture at all.
