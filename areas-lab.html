Areas & Goals

areas-and-goals.md is the only module doc with no lab, and the gap shows in what it contains: a layer the app seeds on day one and never draws again, and a screen that is empty for a fortnight by design.

Its own thesis is that Areas and Goals are "not destinations" — filters and parents. That holds for Areas, which have no detail screen anywhere in the set. It does not hold for Goals, and the two counts that decide the module are both wrong in the same direction: too small.

Where they appear

§Surfaces lists five, and surfaces.md files five. The disagreement is not in the list but in the reach: §Surfaces says the Area filter is "In the Tasks and Habits modes", and §Filters — four paragraphs later, in the same doc — gives it four places.

Checking the four against the modules is the interesting part. Two are real, one is grouping being described as filtering, and one is claimed here and specified nowhere.
