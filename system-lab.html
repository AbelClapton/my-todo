Lab 18 · the last uncovered surface

System states

Four docs describe what the app looks like when something is wrong or missing: error-handling.md, sync-engine.md, local-first.md and states.md. Every other doc in the set has an owner for its surfaces; these four describe surfaces that belong to no module, which is why they went the longest without being drawn.

All four findings are the same shape as ones already fixed elsewhere — a closed list four things sit outside of, a doc cited for something it does not contain, and a number that appears in three places and is defined in none.

Four banners, one slot

app-shell.md gives the shell five layers and puts three things in layer 3: offline, stale calendar, local-only warning. It says "Banners never stack. If two want to show, the higher-priority one wins and the other waits" — and it says, two sections earlier, "Nothing in the shell is a nudge. Banners inform; they do not ask."

That second sentence is the one to check, because it is a property of the container rather than of any banner. Two of the three banners in the layer carry an action, and the copy that specifies them lives in a document that never mentions the layer at all.
