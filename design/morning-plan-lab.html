Lab 13 · the two rituals that bookend a day

Morning plan & shutdown

shutdown.md says of itself: "The shutdown is the mirror of the morning plan. The morning plan commits; the shutdown reviews and pre-commits." Two docs, one pair, and each is specified as if the other were not there.

That is where the defects are. The shutdown's commit writes a plan for tomorrow, and the morning plan's trigger refuses to fire on a day that is already planned. Together they cancel: the nights the user shuts down are the mornings the plan does not appear. Everything else in the lab follows from reading the two docs against each other rather than one at a time.

Does it fire

morning-plan.md §The trigger lists four conditions, each a gate. The invariant above them says it "surfaces once per day, in the first app open after the day boundary", and spec adds a window — "the morning window (default 5am–11am local)".

Two things outside this doc decide the answer. The shutdown flow writes a plan for tomorrow, and one of the four conditions is "the user has not already planned that day". And the attention budget owns the hours, not this doc — which quietly removes the window's first two.

The asymmetry in the log

Changed

Cost

Against rules
Neither doc is wrong alone. shutdown.md means to save the next morning work; morning-plan.md means to plan a day that has not been planned. What it commits

The ritual's entire claim is a number. §Purpose: "It has one job: commit to three things." §The 60-second structure: "Three steps, each optional." §The commit logs "day.planned with the top three."

"The picker shows candidates as a scrollable list with checkboxes. The user taps three. A "Done" button commits."
Changed

Against rules

The swipes

Both rituals put three swipes on a list of task rows: right to keep, left to defer, down to remove. gesture-vocabulary.md is blunt about that kind of thing: "Each gesture means the same thing everywhere it is available. If a gesture is not available on a surface, it is disabled … — not repurposed."

That doc's matrix has thirteen rows. Neither ritual is one of them. So the two surfaces that invent a task-row vocabulary are the two the vocabulary's own table does not list.
