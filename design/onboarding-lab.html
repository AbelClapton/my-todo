Onboarding lab

Seven screens, one at a time. Each screen is a table of rows: one per variation, with the markup on the left and everything that explains it on the right. Every row carries the same three lines — what it changed, what it cost, and which of the app's own rules it breaks. "None" is a finding too, so no row leaves that cell empty.

The fourth row is the recommended one. It is not a fifth option: it takes what the others agreed on, keeps the moves that survived their costs, and states what it still needs before it can ship.

fixed: token set · palette · type scale · Lucide · row heights · motion rules · copy

One consequence worth stating once: none of the three lenses can move type or colour here, so a distinctive visual identity is unavailable by construction. That is the closed token set working as intended — and it is why the differences below are composition rather than style.

Screen 1 — The premise

The spec fixes everything this screen carries: the identity sentence verbatim, one action ("Get started"), one skip ("Just let me in"), a seven-segment indicator, and a top-trailing "Skip" on screens 1–6. There is almost nothing to decide — which is what makes it a good first test of the lenses. What is left is composition, what the indicator means, and whether the screen adds anything at all.

spec tension  "Skip" top-trailing and "Just let me in" are two affordances for the same act on the same screen. Every variation below has to choose: keep both, or keep the one that says what happens.

What the four agree on

The agreements are the strongest signal, because four different lenses arriving at the same answer is not a style preference.

Agreed

  • The sentence is the whole screen. No variation added an image, a logo, or a second idea. V3's legend is the only addition and it reads as the odd one out.
  • One primary action, bottom, full width. All four put "Get started" in the thumb zone at control-lg.
  • The indicator belongs at the top. It survives in all four, as seven segments or as something quieter.
  • Nothing animates on arrival. Consistent with ADR 0019 and with the app having no entrance choreography.

In conflict

  • Does the screen say what happens next? V2 and V3 both decided a bare claim is not enough. V1 and V4 both decided it is. This is the real question on this screen.
  • Do the seven segments mean anything? V1 and V4 keep them as progress. V2 finds them decorative. V3 makes them carry the flow's shape.
  • One skip or two? V1 keeps both because the spec says so. The other three drop the top-trailing one and keep the sentence that states its own consequence.
