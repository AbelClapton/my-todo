Lab 05 · first module outside the Calendar

Habits

The Calendar took eight surfaces because it has eight. Habits has one component and four numbers — and the four numbers disagree with each other.

The row's checkbox has four states. Two of them are drawn as a ring, and their rings are success-default and accent-default. Two others are flat greys. The module's central invariant is "Skipping is not failing" — which is a claim about whether two colours can be told apart. So this lab measures that, and it starts by finding out that the three labs before this one each redefined the same four contrast functions, which is why they now live in lab.js.

The habit row

habits.md §The habit row fixes four elements in 56px: [checkbox] Habit name [compliance] over Cadence · area. The checkbox is tap for a full check, and long-press for a mini-menu offering Minimum and Skip. The chart in the detail distinguishes five states; the row has to distinguish four of them in a 24px box.

The invariant this row is carrying: "The minimum is not a lesser check; it counts as compliance on bad days." That is a statement about how the two checked states should look, and the row's answer is a 1px underline. The chart's answer is a different colour and a ring. Two surfaces, two answers.

Compliance & History

habits.md shows compliance on the row as "18/28" or "64% (default is counts)", and History as "per-habit compliance with a window selector — 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, custom." The chart in the detail is 28 days.

So there are two numerals in the module — 28 in the chart and the row's example — and they are not the same quantity. And the row's 18/28 is arithmetically impossible for a habit created ten days ago. If the denominator is calendar days in a window, a new habit shows 4/28 on its fourth day. If it is scheduled days, a weekly habit shows 2/4 and the strip shows 12 cells. No doc says which.
