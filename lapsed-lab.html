Lab 14 · coming back, and being reminded

Lapsed recovery & resurfacing

lapsed-recovery.md states its rule in four words: "two doors, no guilt. The app never dumps the backlog on someone who is already struggling." It is the most explicitly humane doc in the set, and it is the one that measures worst.

Because resurfacing.md is the doc that lists what the user has neglected — and its thresholds are built from the same numbers as the lapse, so its material is guaranteed to exist on the day the user comes back. Neither doc mentions the other. A second defect sits under the first: the lapse card's trigger is a state that the app destroys while it is deciding.

When it fires

§The trigger is two lines: "Lapse is defined as ≥ 14 days since the last day.opened", and the threshold is configurable. §The screen says the card fires once per lapse and is exempt from the attention budget.

Both are true, and they destroy each other. An exempt surface "still obeys quiet hours, focus mode, and in-event suppression" — and the entry the trigger counts is written by rollover, which is not budget-governed. So the open that reveals the lapse is also the open that erases it.
